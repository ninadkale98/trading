- Lesson of the day: **Bear-market rally (dead cat bounce)**: A sharp but often short-lived rebound inside a falling market. Many of the biggest one-day gains in history happened during downtrends, such as 2008 and 2020, because sellers are exhausted and short sellers rush to buy back. One huge up day shows fear has eased, not that the trend has turned. Look for gains that continue over several days, the average stock joining in (equal-weight keeping pace with cap-weight) and credit markets improving.
