## 5. What the system learned
- No predictions due today.
- Lesson of the day: **Selling the good news (reaction vs. result)**: A stock's price moves on results compared with what investors already expected and owned, not on the results alone. When a sell-off is driven by fear and crowded positions, even beats and raised guidance (Nvidia, Walmart today) can be met with selling. How a stock reacts to news is often a better read on the market's mood than the news itself. If good news can't lift prices, sellers are still in control. If bad news stops pushing prices down, that is often an early sign a bottom is forming.
