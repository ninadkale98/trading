- Lesson of the day: **Fund weighting (why two similar ETFs can move differently)**: A fund's name tells you what it owns, but its weights tell you how much of each stock it holds. Today SOXX rose 7.5% and SMH rose 5.1%, even though both are chip funds. SMH holds much more Nvidia, which fell. When a few giants dominate a fund, their news can outweigh everything else inside it, so check the top holdings before trusting the label.
