- Lesson of the day: **Equal-weight vs cap-weight index**: A cap-weighted index like SPY counts each company by its market value, so a few giants (mostly big tech) drive most of its move. An equal-weight version like RSP gives each of the 500 stocks the same weight, so it shows what the average stock is doing. This month SPY fell 5.6% while RSP rose 1.4%. The headline index looked like a selloff, but the typical stock was holding up. When the two diverge, ask which one describes the market you care about.
